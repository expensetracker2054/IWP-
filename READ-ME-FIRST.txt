IWP — deploy bundle  (v12 · Myanmar text)
=======================================
Upload these INDIVIDUALLY into your existing IWP repo, then commit:

    index.html   manifest.json   sw.js
    icon-192.png   icon-512.png   apple-touch-icon.png

The .github folder has one more file. Drag-and-drop does not handle folders
well, so make that one by hand instead:
    GitHub > Add file > Create new file
    type the name:  .github/copilot-instructions.md
    paste the contents of .github/copilot-instructions.md from this bundle
It tells GitHub Copilot the rules of this repo so it cannot quietly break
your storage key or ship a build your phone will not pick up.

ONE SETTING TO CHANGE IN FIREBASE (once, not per device)
--------------------------------------------------------
Sign-in is now by emailed link. There is no password.
    Firebase console > Authentication > Sign-in method
    > Email/Password > turn ON "Email link (passwordless sign-in)" > Save
Check that expensetracker2054.github.io is listed under
    Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains
It should already be there from the expense tracker.

Signing in (each device)
------------------------
1. Open the app > More > Across your devices
2. If it asks for Firebase settings, paste the ones from daily-spend's
   index.html (apiKey, authDomain, projectId, appId)
3. Type your email > "Email me a sign-in link"
4. Open your inbox ON THAT DEVICE and tap the link
Use the SAME email everywhere and all three devices share one book. It is
the same Firebase account as the expense tracker, so one login covers both.

ON IPHONE / IPAD, USE THE PASTE BOX
-----------------------------------
A Home Screen app on iOS keeps its own login, separate from Safari. Tapping
the link in Mail opens Safari and signs THAT in, not your Home Screen app.
So on iPhone and iPad do this instead:
    open the email > press and hold the link > Copy Link
    open IWP from the Home Screen > More > Across your devices
    paste it into "Paste the link" > Finish signing in
On a PC, just tapping the link works normally.

If you open the link on a different device from the one that asked for it,
the app asks you to confirm the email address. That is expected.
