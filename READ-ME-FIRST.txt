IWP — deploy bundle
===================
Upload these files INDIVIDUALLY (not the zip, not the folder) into your
GitHub repo, then commit:

    index.html   manifest.json   sw.js
    icon-192.png   icon-512.png   apple-touch-icon.png

Every release after this one: bump APP_VER near the top of index.html AND
CACHE at the top of sw.js to the same number, and upload BOTH files. If you
only upload index.html, phones keep serving the old build.

Turning sync on (once per device)
---------------------------------
1. Open daily-spend's index.html and copy the firebaseConfig values
   (apiKey, authDomain, projectId, appId).
2. In IWP: More > Across your devices > paste them > Save and connect.
3. Sign in with the SAME email and password you use for the expense tracker.
   Repeat 2-3 on the iPad and the PC.

No Firestore rules change is needed: the existing users/{uid} rule already
covers this app's path, users/{uid}/iwp_chunks/...

Stay on Firebase's Spark plan. Never click Upgrade to Blaze.
